# Clawbound: The Sunken Vault

## Short description
Scoop your weapons. Forge your build. Crack the vault.

## Description
Pilot a brass salvager into a forgotten mechanical kingdom. Your weapon is a claw machine—and every grab changes your chances of survival.

Aim for the treasure your build needs. Close the claw at the right depth, watch its fingers wrap around the pile, and hold your breath as loose pieces slip. Only loot that reaches the delivery chute powers your machine.

Build your expedition around swords, sparks, shields, healing, or scrap. Face twelve chambers across three acts, read enemy intentions, choose upgrades, and decide when to visit a merchant, recover at a sanctuary, or challenge an elite treasury.

Defeat three guardians and uncover the vault’s power core. Bank earned embers in your workshop, improve future expeditions, and unlock new salvagers.

• Physical, freely tumbling treasure with distinct shapes.
• Drag-to-grab controls and manual closing depth.
• A twelve-chamber campaign with bosses and branching routes.
• Permanent workshop progression and unlockable machines.
• Offline play and automatic saves on your device.
• No accounts, advertisements, or in-app purchases.
