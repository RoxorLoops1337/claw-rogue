Privacy information · Updated 24 September 2026

Clawbound has no accounts, advertising, analytics, or in-app purchases.

Your saved run, unlocks, and settings are stored on your device.

What the game stores

The game uses local device storage to remember your progress, unlocked machines, best results, and sound and accessibility settings. It does not send those saves to a game server. Saves do not automatically transfer between browsers, devices, or the web and native editions.

Removing your data

For the web edition, clear this site's data in your browser settings. For a native installation, clear the app's storage or delete the app. This removes saved progress and settings; it cannot be undone. Your operating system may separately manage device backups under your own backup settings.

Web hosting and external links

The web edition is hosted on GitHub Pages. Loading the website sends ordinary web requests to GitHub, which may process connection information such as IP addresses under its own privacy statement. The native edition bundles the game and its artwork for offline play. Opening a support or other external link is subject to the destination's privacy practices.

Permissions

The game does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, or photos.

Support

You can report a problem through the Clawbound project support page. GitHub issues are public, so please do not include private information in a report.
